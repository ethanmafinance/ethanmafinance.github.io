# Renewables sources (collected 2026-11-17)

## Dufferin Wind (THE RENEWABLES EXAMPLE)
91.4 MW, Dufferin County, Ontario. 49 GE turbines (31 x 1.6 MW, 18 x 2.75 MW), 47 km transmission line. Sponsor China Longyuan Power Group.

D15: DBRS, final rating of Dufferin Wind Power senior secured bonds, Oct 2015. https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/285877
- COD under the FIT contract Dec 1, 2014; FIT expires Nov 30, 2034, 12 months after scheduled full repayment of the bonds
- 4.317% senior secured bonds
- Effective energy price $146.60/MWh, indexed at 20% of Ontario CPI
- IE one-year P90 251.6 GWh (31.4% capacity factor); DBRS rating case 3% below that
- Rating case minimum and average semi-annual DSCR 1.60x and 1.64x (provisional release 284823 said 1.60x and 1.63x)

D20R: DBRS rating report Sep 2020. https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/367761
- $200 million bonds issued Oct 22, 2015; about $161.5m outstanding; due Nov 30, 2033

D20: DBRS confirmation Sep 25, 2020. https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/367282
- 2019: 278.6 GWh at average realized $148.52/MWh; DSCR 2.02x vs 1.61x rating case
- One-year P90 rating case 241.6 GWh; P50 plan 289.2 GWh; rating case min and avg 1.61x and 1.64x

D23: DBRS confirmation Oct 11, 2023. https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/421700
- 2022: 275.7 GWh at $151.44/MWh; DSCR 2.00x vs 1.62x rating case; about $127.46m outstanding; P50 plan 272.43 GWh

D24: Morningstar DBRS confirmation Oct 7, 2024. https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/440874
- 2023: 226.9 GWh at $153.52/MWh; DSCR 1.63x vs 1.62x; about $123.8m outstanding; P50 plan 265.5 GWh
- Average generation about 10.4% above the 241.6 GWh rating case since COD

Not public: project cost, operating costs, tax position, reserve and distribution terms, the repayment schedule.

## Calibration (2026-11-17)
One unknown solved: operating costs C$2.39m/yr in 2015 dollars (about C$26/kW-yr), so the rating case at a 1.60x minimum DSCR supports $200.0m of level-payment debt over 18 years at 4.317%. The tax pool is set to fully used by 2016 because any remaining pool lifts early rating-case coverage well above the flat 1.60x to 1.64x published. Read the cost as net of whatever the rating case assumes for tax; it is not a bottom-up O&M estimate.

Results vs published: bonds 200.0 vs 200 (0.0%); Sep 2020 balance 163.1 vs 161.5 (+1.0%, 2% tolerance because DBRS says "approximately"); Oct 2024 balance 124.1 vs 123.8 (+0.2%); rating case min DSCR 1.60x vs 1.60x; average 1.63x vs 1.64x (-0.8%); realized price 2019 149.0 vs 148.52 (+0.3%); 2022 151.0 vs 151.44 (-0.3%).
Not used: the Oct 2023 balance of 127.46. It implies only $3.7m repaid in the next 12 months against about $9m a year before, so it conflicts with the 2024 figure. 2023 realized price 153.52 vs model 151.6 (-1.2%), likely because actual 2021 to 2022 inflation ran above the flat 2% assumption (inference; no CPI source opened). Recorded here, not used as a check.
Model outputs (est., driven by the K2-based build cost): equity IRR 12.5%, project IRR 6.9%, MOIC 2.82x.
