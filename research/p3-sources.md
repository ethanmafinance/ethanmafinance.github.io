## Humber River Hospital (the P3 example)
Source H1: Infrastructure Ontario, "Making Projects Happen: Humber River Regional Hospital" value for money report.
https://www.infrastructureontario.ca/48e23c/contentassets/e94995ec4cc94042a6f76fd0eca4d39c/hrrh-vfm-report.pdf
- Commercial and financial close: September 26, 2011 (p. 12)
- Construction December 2011 to spring 2015; "approximately 44 months of construction and a 30-year maintenance timeframe" (p. 13)
- Maintenance summer 2015 to fall 2045 (p. 13)
- Substantial completion payment $611.5 million; service payments "an average of $53.9 million each year for a 30-year period" (p. 14)
- AFP base costs $1.76 billion vs $1.27 billion traditional; risks retained $1.17 billion traditional vs $201 million AFP; PSC $2.45 billion vs AFP $1.98 billion; value for money $469 million (19.1%) (pp. 18 to 20)
- Consortium: Plenary Health Care Partnerships (Plenary Group/HCP Canada, PCL, HDR, C.F. Moller, Johnson Controls, Hewlett-Packard, Plan Group, Modern Niagara, Smith+Andersen, Halsall, RBC Capital Markets) (p. 12)

Source H2: DBRS, "DBRS Assigns Provisional Rating of A to Plenary Health Care Partnerships Humber LP, Trend Stable", August 31, 2011. Figures in brackets in the release (provisional).
https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/241811
- Fixed-price date-certain construction contract $974.8 million; start September 23, 2011; substantial completion May 11, 2015; 43.5 months
- Short-term senior bonds [$482.089m]; Series A long-term senior bonds [$373.437m]; Series B long-term senior bonds [$149.010m]
- DSCR [1.25]x foreseen over the term; debt to CFADS [11.8]x in the first operating year
- Initial gearing 92.6%, 86.6% at substantial completion
- Lifecycle reserve "in excess of [$10.5] million"; look-forward inspections years 15, 18, 21

Source H3: Morningstar DBRS confirmation at A (low), negative trends (research/450584): DSCR 1.25x for the 12 months ended Nov 30, 2024, the minimum expected over the life.
https://dbrs.morningstar.com/research/450584

Source H5: Plenary project page (opened 2026-11-17), https://plenary.com/project/humber-river-hospital : "a short bond, paid out at substantial completion", plus a long amortizing bond and a long bullet bond; NPV C$1.75bn; contract 33.5 years. No coupons or amounts.

Calibration (2026-11-17): coupons, payment split and non-construction costs are not public. Four inputs are solved together so the model hits all five published figures: capital payment 48.05 (nominal, fixed), services payment 4.0 (start-year $, CPI), blended bond rate 5.08%, other project costs 157.6. Result: LT debt 522.4, debt/CFADS 11.80x, DSCR 1.25x every year, gearing 86.6%, average payment 53.9. Cross-check: 5.08% blended is just above the 4.895% Cbonds coupon, consistent with a longer bullet tranche at a higher coupon (inference). Model outputs (not published): equity IRR 7.2%, project IRR 4.7%, MOIC 2.73x, with equity funded pro rata during construction.
