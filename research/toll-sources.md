# Toll road sources (collected 2026-11-17)

## Confederation Bridge, Strait Crossing Development Inc. (SCDI) (THE TOLL EXAMPLE)
12.9 km bridge, New Brunswick to Prince Edward Island. Opened May 31, 1997. SCDI operates it until 2032, then it transfers to the Government of Canada (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederation_Bridge). Debt: 6.17% Revenue Bonds, rated by DBRS. Tolls rise at most 75% of national CPI under the concession.

DBRS press releases (all opened 2026-11-17):
- 241882 (Sep 7, 2011): 2010 DSCR 1.13x DBRS (1.10x indenture). DSRA plus GRA cover nearly two years of debt service.
- 262320 (Nov 1, 2013): debt $285.0m at Sep 30, 2013, down 2.0% from a year before; "accretive amortization of the bonds"; 2012 DSCR 1.17x.
- 274799 (Dec 8, 2014): debt $278.5m at Sep 30, 2014; 2013 DSCR 1.13x; tolls capped at 75% of national CPI.
- 299069 (Sep 6, 2016): 2015 traffic 766,921 vehicles; 2015 DSCR 1.19x DBRS (1.14x MTI); toll revenue $15.8m for the period to Jun 30, 2016.
- 319149 (Nov 16, 2017): debt $254.3m at Sep 30, 2017; 2016 traffic 837,755; 2016 DSCR 1.35x DBRS (1.24x MTI); $0.50 two-axle toll increase.
- 335988 (Nov 2018): debt $244.6m at Sep 30, 2018; 2017 traffic 865,738; 2017 DSCR 1.39x DBRS (1.33x MTI); $0.50 two-axle increase.
- 352846 (Nov 2019): debt $233.8m at Sep 30, 2019; 2018 traffic 864,434; 2018 DSCR 1.40x DBRS (1.35x MTI); $0.75 two-axle increase.
- 361207 (2020): 2019 traffic 887,882, up 2.7% on 2018; six-month DSRA fully funded.
- 388869 (Nov 30, 2021): 2020 traffic 56% of 2019; revenue down almost 40%.
- 424378 (Nov 28, 2023): 2022 DSCR 1.37x; toll freeze from Jan 1, 2023 at 2022 rates; $2.5m government assistance in Jan 2023.
- 443559 (Nov 27, 2024): 2023 DSCR 1.62x (rating case 1.50x); 2024 expected 1.70x; 2025 rating case 1.52x; 2023 traffic 7% above 2019; 2013 to 2023 traffic CAGR 2.6%; freeze continued in 2024 with $4.5m assistance; reserves about $27m, about one year of debt service.

Tolls (two-axle vehicle):
- Trucknews, Dec 18, 2014: $45.50 from Jan 1, 2015 (+$0.25); $7.50 per additional axle. https://www.trucknews.com/transportation/confederation-bridge-tolls-rise-for-second-year-in-a-row/1003111233
- Chain from DBRS: +$0.50 (2017 release), +$0.50 (2018 release), +$0.75 (2019 release). With $47.75 for 2019 (CBC, search excerpt only; page blocked), 2018 was $47.00 and 2017 $46.50 (inference).
- Wikipedia: $50.25 by January 2022, then frozen; cut to $20 from Aug 1, 2025, "further subsidized by the Federal Government".

Not public: bond issue size, maturity, repayment schedule, revenue, operating costs, tax position, equity value or owners' cost.

## Calibration (2026-11-17)
Model starts at Sep 30, 2017 (debt $254.3m); model years run Oct to Sep. Inferences:
- Debt service grows 2% a year: principal repaid was $9.7m (2017 to 2018) and $10.8m (2018 to 2019); with 6.17% interest that implies debt service of $25.4m then $25.9m, up 2.0%.
- Tenor 14 years (repaid by Sep 2031, inside the concession). 13 years fits the balances equally; 14 gives 2024 debt service of $27.8m, closer to the "about $27m, about one year of debt service" reserve.
- Traffic growth 1.91% a year from 2018 so 2023 traffic equals 1.07 x 2019 (DBRS). The model skips the 2020 to 2022 pandemic drop.
- Average toll per vehicle taken as the two-axle toll; trucks pay more, so the solved operating cost ($6.10m in 2017 dollars) is a net figure that makes the 2018 DSCR 1.40x. Tax set to 0: the solved cost is net of whatever tax the DBRS DSCR reflects.
- Debt sized at 1.40x on the first year with no lender haircut, which reproduces $254.3m.

Results: debt 254.3 vs 254.3; Sep 2018 balance 245.3 vs 244.6 (+0.3%); Sep 2019 235.2 vs 233.8 (+0.6%); 2018 DSCR 1.40x vs 1.40x; 2019 traffic 880.9k vs 887.9k (-0.8%); 2022 toll $50.08 vs $50.25 (-0.3%).
Shown as not used: 2023 DSCR 1.62x actual vs model 1.52x (2023 actual includes $2.5m of freeze compensation and post-pandemic traffic); DBRS 2023 rating case 1.50x vs 1.52x and 2025 rating case 1.52x vs 1.57x (DBRS's own traffic case, not public).
Equity: the price SCDI's owners paid is not public, so the site shows equity value at a buyer's target IRR instead of an IRR.
